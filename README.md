💸 Expense Tracker

Take control of your finances effortlessly!
The Expense Tracker is a user-friendly web application built with Flask that helps you track your income and expenses.
Whether you like to manually log transactions or import them in bulk from a CSV file, this app gives you instant insights into your spending habits, including monthly breakdowns and a calendar view to visualize your financial activity.
Perfect for students, professionals, or anyone who wants to stay on top of their budget without complicated tools.

<br><br>
✨ Features

📝 Manual Entry: Quickly add your income or expense transactions.

📂 CSV Import: Upload your transactions directly from a CSV file.

📅 Calendar View: Visualize your spending patterns day-by-day.

📊 Monthly Breakdown: See a detailed summary of income vs. expenses per month.

🔒 Secure: Optional login system using Flask-Login (if implemented).

💡 Insightful Analytics: Helps you identify spending trends and manage your money better.


<br><br>
🛠️ Tech Stack

Backend: Flask, Flask-SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Database: SQLite (easily switchable to PostgreSQL/MySQL)
